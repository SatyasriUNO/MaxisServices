Our Journey

Building trust, delivering excellence since 2009

Welcome to Maxis Services

Maxis Services is India's leading electrical goods distributor, committed to delivering premium quality products and exceptional service to businesses across the nation. With over 15 years of industry experience, we have built a reputation for reliability, innovation, and customer-centric solutions. Our extensive network of channel partners and clients trusts us to power their businesses with superior electrical distribution services.

Our Mission

To be the most trusted and innovative electrical goods distributor in India, empowering businesses through quality products, reliable services, and lasting partnerships that drive mutual growth and success.

Our Vision

To create a comprehensive ecosystem of electrical goods distribution that connects quality manufacturers with businesses nationwide, fostering innovation, sustainability, and shared prosperity across the entire supply chain.

Our Values

Integrity: Honest dealings in all business. Excellence: Quality in every product. Innovation: Continuous improvement. Partnership: Mutual growth with stakeholders.

Leadership

Founder Photo

Rajesh Kumar Sharma

Founder & CEO

Rajesh Kumar Sharma founded Maxis Services in 2009 with a vision to revolutionize electrical goods distribution in India. With a background in electrical engineering and business management, he brings two decades of industry expertise and strategic vision to the company.

Under his leadership, Maxis Services has grown from a small distributor to become one of India's most respected names in electrical goods supply. Rajesh's commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has established Maxis as the go-to partner for businesses nationwide.

Company Milestones

2009 - Foundation

Maxis Services founded with mission to revolutionize electrical goods distribution across India with premium quality products.

2012 - Regional Expansion

Successfully expanded operations to Western and Southern India, establishing strong distribution networks in major cities.

2015 - National Presence

Achieved pan-India distribution network with partnerships in all major states, becoming a national leader in electrical goods supply.

2018 - Technology Integration

Launched digital platform for seamless ordering and tracking, introducing modern technology to traditional distribution.

2021 - ISO Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification, reinforcing our commitment to quality management and customer satisfaction.

2024 - Future Ready

Continuing innovation with sustainable practices and expanded product portfolio to meet evolving market demands.
