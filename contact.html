Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deliver?

We offer delivery within 24-72 hours for most products in major cities. Remote areas may take 5-7 business days.

What are your payment options?

We accept bank transfers, cheques, credit/debit cards, UPI, and cash on delivery for qualified customers. Credit terms available for established partners.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes, we provide volume-based discounts for bulk orders. Please contact our sales team to discuss your requirements.

What warranty do products have?

All products come with manufacturer's warranty. Specific warranty terms depend on the product. Our support team can provide detailed warranty information.

How do I become a partner?

Visit our Partners page to learn about requirements and submit an application through our online form.
